Exhibitions and Displays

Our distinctive and inclusive exhibitions programme is based on or around works of art from the Collection, our history, and the areas of art and history best represented in the Wallace Collection. You can also enjoy our exceptional series of rolling one-room displays in the Housekeeper's Room.

Winston Churchill: The Painter

Join us for a major retrospective of Sir Winston Churchill's paintings.

Metamorphoses: Porcelain Sculpture by Rachel Kneebone

Discover Rachel Kneebone's striking sculptures in our free display.

Explore Cosimo I de' Medici's world of art and power in our upcoming free display.
