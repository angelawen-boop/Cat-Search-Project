Current exhibitions

  • Musée des Arts Décoratifs

    La Mode en Majesté. Royal Thai Dress from Tradition to Modernity from 13 May to 1 November 2026 The Musée des Arts Décoratifs presents a unique exhibition on the clothing evolution at the Thai court, featuring pieces from the royal collection.

  • Musée des Arts Décoratifs

    A Day in the Eighteenth Century: Chronicle of a Parisian Townhouse from 18 February to 4 October 2026 The Musée des Arts Décoratifs invites visitors to step inside the intimate world of an eighteenth-century aristocratic residence and its inhabitants – masters, servants, and household animals.
