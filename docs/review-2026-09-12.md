# Her review of the 13 unchecked venues — 12 Sep 2026

She reviewed the scraper's output against the live sites herself. **These are her
findings, not a session's guesses.** They are the input to the fixing work. Do not
ask her to re-review a venue recorded here.

Scope: the 12 venues marked "fixes pending" in the project guide's §2 table.
`borghese` is NOT in this review — she has not re-reviewed it since 7 Sep and the
engine has changed since.

Reading these notes: a heading marked **COUNT CORRECT** means she has verified the
number of rows the scraper produced against the live site. A blank line under a
topic with no comment means she found no problem with that topic.

## Her overall verdict across the 12

- **Dates and titles — generally good.**
- **Not pulling in non-exhibitions — generally good.**
- **Pagination — isolated misses on a few sites**, listed per venue below, PLUS one
  general concern (next bullet).
- **Year-archive addresses, her general concern:** where a venue changes year with a
  drop-down that changes the page address, she believed the recipes wire in 2025 and
  2024 by hand and would silently stop covering 2026, 2027 and onward.
  **Checked 12 Sep: already solved, no work needed.** No recipe contains a year.
  `expandYearArchive()` derives them at run time from the lookback floor's year up to
  last year, so the set widens on its own as time passes. Fixtures Y-001 to Y-005
  hold the line. Reported back to her.
- **Biggest issue: junk text in the summaries.** Picture captions, "join us on X for
  the opening talk", ticketing information. Two distinct harms, and the second is the
  serious one:
  1. Noise the compression model should be able to ignore.
  2. Text the model **cannot tell apart from the blurb** — an artist's name in a photo
     caption reads exactly like an artist named in the exhibition text, so compression
     can state something false with no way to know.
- **Some summaries are ticketing information and nothing else.**

### Menil — COUNT WRONG, 12 rows missing

| | |
|---|---|
| Her count | 32 — 5 current, 4 upcoming, **23 past** |
| Scraper returned | 20 — 5 current, 4 upcoming, **11 past** |
| Titles, dates, summaries, site access | no issues found |

**The defect: `/exhibitions/past` is paginated and only its first page is read.**
11 rows on the bare address, 12 more on `?page=1`. Her oldest expected row is
*Ruth Asawa*, 22 Mar – 21 Jul 2024 — which sits inside the lookback, so all 12 are
genuinely lost, not filtered.

**Her ruling, confirmed:** dropping Menil rows with no dates at all is correct here —
those are the permanent collection galleries. `excludeUndated` stays.

**Fixed 12 Sep:** `followPagination()` now walks the archive to its true end —
11 pages at this venue — and the verification sweep returned **32 rows, her count
exactly**, ending on *Ruth Asawa Through Line*, 22 Mar – 21 Jul 2024, the boundary
row she named.

**Found by the verification run, not by her review — the undated rule is doing the
right thing for the wrong reason.** Reading all 11 archive pages instead of one
turned 7 undated rows into 20, and only the first 7 are the permanent galleries
her ruling was about (*Modern and Contemporary*, *Surrealism*, *Africa*,
*4th–18th Century Europe*, *Pacific Islands*, *A Surrealist Wunderkammer*,
*Outdoor Sculpture*). The other 13 are genuinely old exhibitions off the deep
archive pages — *Andy Warhol: Shadows*, *Marcel Duchamp / Fountain*, *Men's Lives*,
*Texas Art*, *John Chamberlain* — for which the Menil publishes no dates at all.
The rule's stated basis, "the seven with no date are precisely the seven
galleries", was true of one archive page and is not true of eleven.

Half of that is now fixed and half is hers to decide:

- **Fixed: a row whose own page FAILED to load is never excluded.** One Menil
  detail page timed out twice on the verification run and its exhibition was filed
  as a permanent display. An empty pair of date columns only means "the venue
  published no run" if the page was actually read; when it was not, we know
  nothing, and the row was being dropped on the strength of a network timeout —
  invisibly, because a CSV cannot show a row that is not in it. Such rows are now
  kept and carry the note explaining why.
- **Open for her: the 13 old undated exhibitions.** Keeping them is what the
  standing lookback rule says (an unknown date is not evidence of being too old)
  and costs her 13 cards to reject on every sweep, forever. Dropping them is
  almost certainly right in fact — they are decades old — but the scraper cannot
  prove it. **Not decided, not implemented.**
