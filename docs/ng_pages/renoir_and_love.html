    Renoir and Love

    3 October 2026 – 31 January 2027

    Fall in love with Renoir and his Impressionist paintings at the National Gallery. 

    Discover Renoir’s gift for showing love, friendship, and everyday emotion in this Impressionist exhibition. See couples waltzing in the summer air and lovers stealing a glance, families sharing a quiet moment and friends raising a glass, all through the eyes of an artist unmatched in capturing the complexities of how people connect. 

    Experience Renoir at his peak in the 1860s to 1880s, experimenting with style and theme to become an innovator of Impressionism.  

    Walk with him through Montmartre in his iconic painting 'Bal au Moulin de la Galette' (1876) from the Musée d’Orsay, Paris, on display in the UK for the first time. Explore the sun-dappled gardens and swirling dance halls of Paris through 40 paintings, brought together from collections across the world. 

    Linked through friendship and love, enjoy the tenderness and joy of some of Renoir’s most modern, uplifting and admired paintings. 


    Exhibition organised by the Musée d’Orsay, the National Gallery and the Museum of Fine Arts, Boston.

    All about Renoir

    One of the founders of Impressionism, Renoir is one of the greatest painters of 19th-century art.

    Watch films that celebrate his fascination with love and human interaction. Hear from experts about the first 20 years of his career and immerse yourself in some of his most famous paintings.

    Pay What You Wish

    Each Friday evening from 23 October 2026 to 15 January 2027, we invite visitors to pay what they wish for this exhibition.

    This means you can pay as little as £1 or as much as you’d like to enjoy the tenderness and joy of some of Renoir’s most modern, uplifting and admired paintings.
