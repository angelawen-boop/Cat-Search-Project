Terry Winters: Vitalized Geometry

Installation view of Terry Winters: Vitalized Geometry at the Menil Collection, Houston. Photo: Paul Hester

September 25, 2026–April 4, 2027

Main Building
About Objects Stories Events
Gallery guide

Terry Winters: Vitalized Geometry presents 44 prints and works on paper by the American artist Terry Winters, who draws inspiration from mathematical systems and structures of the natural world. Celebrating two groups of gifts to the Menil and organized in close consultation with the artist, the career-spanning presentation explores the importance of printmaking to Winters’s practice and its dialogue with other forms of making.  

“

Each medium is an instrument. Almost in a musical sense, meant to be played; and each affects the developing imagery. How they look, how they feel, and what they mean is conditioned by the materials and procedures. Media is used to embody an image actual size, as vitalized geometry—hopefully with a sense of life.

Terry Winters

Terry Winters: Vitalized Geometry is curated by Sophie Asakura, Curatorial Associate, The Menil Collection.

Support

This exhibition is generously supported by Sheila Noeth and Ted Dohmen; Eddie Allen and Chinhui Juhn; Barbara and Michael Gamson; Linda and George Kelly; Karen and Harry Pinson; Karlsson and Brian Salek; Wawro-Gray Family Foundation; and the City of Houston through Houston Arts Alliance.
