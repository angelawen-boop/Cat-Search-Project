Modern and Contemporary Craft

July 27, 2024–February 22, 2026

Gallery 222 & 223
Today’s multifaceted contemporary art scene values the use of any and all media to convey ideas that communicate and reflect upon our shared human experiences. Some artists play to their chosen material’s inherent properties and history while others ingeniously manipulate and bend those attributes to serve their creative goals.

This gallery showcases selections from the collection gifted by Cincinnatians Nancy and David Wolf and other recent acquisitions to our collection. Created over the last seven decades by artists representing seven global nationalities, these artworks highlight the use of glass, ceramic, fiber, and wood to explore themes including human emotion, storytelling, and respect for our natural world.
