Nancy Rexroth: Secrets of My Power

October 2, 2026– January 3, 2027

Free admission
Friends of Photography
Press Release

Nancy Rexroth: Secrets of My Power is the first career retrospective on the work of the American photographer revered for her singular and evocative 1977 photobook, IOWA. The exhibition and accompanying catalogue mark the public debut of The Nancy Rexroth Collection, an archive recently formed at the Cincinnati Art Museum that allows unprecedented and comprehensive insight into the artist’s life and work. Over 150 rare or unique photographs, personal effects, tools, and numerous previously unseen artworks tell a rich and surprising story of Rexroth’s powerful, unique, and steadfastly defiant vision, from the 1960s, 70s, and 80s to the early digital era. 

Nancy Rexroth: Secrets of My Power is organized by the Cincinnati Art Museum. A fully illustrated exhibition catalogue is available for purchase beginning October 2026.

Accessing the Exhibition

Elevators A and B provide the most direct access to this second-floor exhibition. Visitors enter and exit the exhibition via a ramp located in Gallery 234. Learn more about navigating to Gallery 234.

A Welcome Cart in the first room of exhibition offers many tools for your visit, including large-print label booklets. Seating is provided in the gallery. An accessible audio tour is available.

Exhibition Catalog

Nancy Rexroth: Secrets of My Power

Order Now

The first major study of Nancy Rexroth, the richly illustrated, 224-page Secrets of My Power exhibition catalogue furnishes a missing account of Rexroth’s life, work, and career as she established her artistic voice in the experimental 1970s and challenged prevailing approaches to photographic image-making.

Tracing Rexroth’s career from her first photographs to her discovery of the Diana camera and through her exploration of digital tools in the early 2000s, the catalogue helps to distinguish and connect Rexroth to key artistic formations of the American photography world in the 1960s, 70s and 80s, including contested social and creative terrain around feminism and gender, technical experimentation, engagement with language, artistic networks, and photography’s emergence in universities, museums and the art market.

Edited by Nathaniel M. Stein, Curator of Photography, Cincinnati Art Museum.
Contributions by Molly Kalkstein, Gary and Ellen Davis Curator of Photography at the Herbert F. Johnson Museum, Cornell University, NY; Charlotte Ogorek, Rexroth Project Assistant, Cincinnati Art Museum; Nancy Rexroth, artist; John Rohrbach, Curator Emeritus, formerly Senior Curator of Photographs, Amon Carter Museum of American Art, TX; and Nathaniel M. Stein.

 

Nancy Rexroth: Secrets of My Power is a featured exhibition for the 2026 FotoFocus Biennial: The Long View. Now in its eighth iteration, the Biennial activates over 70 exhibitions at museums, galleries, universities, and public spaces throughout Greater Cincinnati, Dayton, Columbus, and Northern Kentucky in October 2026—the largest of its kind in America. The Long View theme considers aspects of time and perspective in photography and film, and how these mediums shape our understanding of the world.


Nancy Rexroth: Secrets of My Power is funded in part by the Millstone Fund, FotoFocus, and the America 250-Ohio Commission.
