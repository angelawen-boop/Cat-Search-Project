Carel Visser

In the Rijksmuseum Gardens

This summer, the Rijksmuseum Gardens are home to the work of Carel Visser, the most influential Dutch sculptor of the twentieth century.

  • 5 June to 25 October
  • Free admission

Carel Visser (1928–2015) had little use for traditional sculpting materials like marble, stone or wood

With a cutting torch and welding equipment, he shaped sculptures from steel plates and beams

Visser always looked to nature and sought its essence

Much of his work stands in public spaces: on squares, in parks and city centres across the Netherlands

In this exhibition, thirteen works come together for the first time
