Exhibition

Giorgio Armani. Milano, per amore

Extended until May 3, 2026 Pinacoteca di Brera


To celebrate fifty years of creativity, the Pinacoteca di Brera presents its first-ever exhibition dedicated to the stylistic journey of Giorgio Armani, welcoming a curated selection of garments into its prestigious galleries.

Over 120 creations trace Armani’s style while reimagining the path through the museum. Painting and fashion intertwine, inviting visitors to engage with unexpected visual and material juxtapositions. 


Giorgio Armani has often spoken of his love for Brera, the neighbourhood he chose to live and work in, and whose dual soul, both cultured and deeply vital, he admired, with its blend of elegance and artistic freedom.

Info

When
From September 24, 2025, to January 11, 2026 Extended until May 3, 2026

Reduced admission to €6,00 upon presentation of a Pinacoteca di Brera ticket. Discount available on-site, through the call center, and through the Armani/Silos website.
